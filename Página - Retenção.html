  • How long does the introductory 0% APR last?

    For 21 full months from account opening (or from the date of the qualifying transfer, if applicable). After that, a variable APR applies based on your credit profile and market rates.

  • Is there a yearly fee?

    No annual fee. That helps you keep more of your savings from the 0% APR period.

  • What does phone protection cover?

    Eligible phones on a qualifying plan may be covered for accidental damage (e.g., cracks, spills). Coverage limits, deductibles, and exclusions apply—check the benefit guide for details.

  • What’s the application process like?

    It’s streamlined and digital. You’ll provide basic personal and financial information, agree to a credit check, and receive a decision—often in minutes.

  • How do I access customized offers?

    Once approved, you may receive personalized offers based on your spending patterns—helping you optimize rewards and savings on categories you use most.

  • Is there a balance transfer fee?

    Many 0% APR balance transfer offers include a fee (commonly a percentage of the amount transferred). Factor this into your payoff plan to confirm the net savings.

  • What credit profile is typically required?

    Issuers generally look for a good to excellent credit profile, stable income, and responsible credit use. Final decisions depend on the issuer’s underwriting.
